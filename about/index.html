Methodology

  • Engine: axe-core 4.14.1, unmodified, served from this site.
  • Rules run: those tagged for the chosen WCAG version and level (70 for WCAG 2.2 AA).
  • Impact order: critical, serious, moderate, minor, as reported by axe-core.
  • Automated testing cannot confirm conformance. Keyboard, screen reader and content checks remain manual.

A11yCheck runs the open-source axe-core 4.14.1 engine (MPL-2.0, Deque Systems) on HTML you paste, inside a sandboxed frame in your browser. Automated rules catch only part of WCAG; manual testing is still needed.
